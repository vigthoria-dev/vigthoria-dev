# Support & Feedback for Vigthoria

We do not track bugs, issues, or feature requests on GitHub. 

To get support, report vulnerabilities, or request new features for the **Operator, Coder, or GoA layers**, please use our official channels directly on our landing platform:

*   💬 **Community Discussions:** [community.vigthoria.io](https://community.vigthoria.io)
*   🛡️ **Official Support & Docs:** [landing.vigthoria.io/docs](https://landing.vigthoria.io)
*   📖 **Main Platform:** [landing.vigthoria.io](https://landing.vigthoria.io)

Please do not open GitHub Issues or Pull Requests in this organization, as they are not monitored by our core dev team.
